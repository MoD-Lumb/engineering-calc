## Definition

Normal (axial) stress is the internal force per unit area acting perpendicular to a cross-section of a member loaded along its centroidal axis.

<Formula tex="\sigma = \dfrac{F}{A}" />

## Variables

| Symbol | Meaning              | Unit |
| ------ | -------------------- | ---- |
| σ      | Normal stress        | MPa  |
| F      | Axial force          | N    |
| A      | Cross-sectional area | mm²  |

## Assumptions

- Linear-elastic material behaviour (Hooke's law).
- Uniform stress distribution across the section — valid far from points of load application (Saint-Venant's principle).
- Prismatic member, load aligned with the centroidal axis (no eccentricity, no bending).
- Cross-section remains plane after loading.

## Sign convention

Tension is positive, compression is negative. The same formula applies to both, with the sign of `F` carried into `σ`.

## Useful relationships

For a member of length **L** and modulus of elasticity **E**, the elastic axial elongation is:

<Formula tex="\Delta L = \dfrac{F \cdot L}{A \cdot E}" />
