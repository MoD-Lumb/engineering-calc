## Example 1 — Bar in tension

A round steel bar of diameter **d = 12 mm** is loaded in pure tension with a force **F = 25 kN**. Find the normal stress.

**Cross-section area:**

<Formula tex="A = \dfrac{\pi d^{2}}{4} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 12^{2}}{4} \approx 113.1\ \text{mm}^{2}" />

**Normal stress:**

<Formula tex="\sigma = \dfrac{F}{A} = \dfrac{25\,000}{113.1} \approx 221\ \text{MPa}" />

For S235 structural steel (`f_y = 235 MPa`), this is just below yield — the member would be at the elastic limit.

## Example 2 — Quick sanity check

A force of **1 kN = 1000 N** acting on **1 cm² = 100 mm²** gives:

<Formula tex="\sigma = \dfrac{1000}{100} = 10\ \text{MPa}" />

Useful rule of thumb: **1 kN per 1 cm² ≈ 10 MPa**.
